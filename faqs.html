Next Stop Packers Movers hereby grants you access to https://www.nextstoppackersmovers.com/ (“the Website”) and invites you to purchase the services offered here.

How would I know my fare charges & bill amount?

You can directly call on our organization's numbers (7200889979/ 9840016441) to request a Quotation or you can just visit our website (www.nextstoppackersmovers.com) to request a quotation. Our customer service employee will send you a quotation immediately as soon as you place the request and explain the procedure to you.

Why do I need to pay a token amount for booking confirmation ? What are the different modes of payment accepted by you?

We take a token amount for booking confirmation as per the agreement. It will be deducted from the total amount specified in the invoice. We accept the token amount transferred in the company's account only which can be made via Gpay, Phonepe or Account transfer.

Can I place a booking with multi-point pick-up and drop?

You can definitely do so. However, extra costs for the extra point of delivery or pick-up will be levied accordingly. Our team will provide you with the additional cost incurred at the time of booking.

How can I cancel my booking? Will I be charged a cancellation fee?

First of all, you are required to raise a cancellation request through the customer care number available on our website. Cancellation will then be approved as per the terms and conditions. A cancellation fee would also be applicable.

Do you also provide loading/unloading & packing services?

Yes. Next Stop Packers Movers provide all types of transportation services such as Packing, Loading, Unloading, Transportation, Insurance, Warehousing, etc.

How many days or hours in advance do I need to make my booking?

You need to make an advance booking at least one or two day prior to the time of pick up.

What are the Payment terms?

In case of Advance booking customers have to pay a token amount via Gpay/NEFT/ IMPS/ PAYTM/ACCOUNT TRANSFER for booking confirmation. Once the truck arrives at the pickup location, customers have to pay 80% at the time of pick up and balance amount at the time of arrival before unloading.

The estimated fare shown on the website is the Final Fare ?

No, the Website/ App gives Estimate cost & the final fare will be shared by our executive after verification of your pickup and drop location, weight, material details etc.

Who will contact me for my booking or after placing a call back request ?

One of our executives will call you back to verify your information. After confirmation of details, the customer has to deposit the booking/ token amount only in Next Stop Packers Movers account for final vehicle placement. For any queries related to booking kindly email us at contact@nextstoppackersmovers.com or call at 7200889979/ 9840016441.

What are the additional charges or waiting charges ?

If the truck has reached client location and there is delay in loading per day, waiting charges range from 1000 to 5000 depending on vehicle type , if the vehicle is not loaded and returned empty, the customer is liable to pay driver fuel charges and waiting charges.

How will I receive my invoice?

You will receive an auto-generated invoice on your registered e-mail ID once the consignment has been delivered. You will also get a hardcopy of the invoice at the time of delivery.

In case there is damage to the goods under transit, what kinds of settlement/procedures are followed by M/s Next Stop Packers Movers?

Next Stop Packers Movers adopt all safety measures to avoid any kind of damage to your goods. However, in case of any unpredictable mishap,we ensure our client speedy settlement of their claims from the insurance company with whom we have long association and understanding.

I am worried about my perishable food items. How M/s Next Stop Packers Movers is going to handle and preserve the same?

Next Stop Packers Movers advises the clients not to shift fast-perishable and food-items such as Vegetables, Fruits, Flowers, Ghee, Pickles etc. as these may spoil your other valuable items. However, we do make arrangements for shifting grocery household items in sealed boxes as per the requirements of the clients.

Can you hold the consignment at the destination for a week, if so what are the charges.?

My Family wishes to get our car loaded in our presence. Is it feasible?

Yes, Next Stop Packers Movers values the client and his precious belongings. We have GPS enabled vehicle carriers to load the car in your presence and similarly deliver it to your new destination.
