How The Amount Is Charged By Packers?

Amount charged from the clients/customers broadly depends on these below mentioned factors viz.
  1. Distance between the pick up point and destination. Local shifting usually is far less expensive than intercity moving as the distance between
  2. No. of items to be transited.
  3. Amount of Manpower involved. Incase of moving from or to a building, the floor also plays a significant role. As it decides the amount of manpower required for the shifting. For instance, a transit from 4th floor of a building to 7th floor of the oher building(no lift) would charge more as compared to transit from 1st floor of a building to 2nd floor of another building (provided there are no lifts in these buildings). In case of lifts involved the charges are reduced.
  4. In some cases the shifting date may also be a reason for varied charges.This can be explained by this example. Let us assume that you have to transit your goods on 16/xx/xxxx but the company already has other shiftings on that day so they may not be able to give you more reasonable prices as even if they get your packing they would have to hire laborers from outside which will reduce their profit. But if they don't have any fixed packings on the next day (17/xx/xxxx) then the chances of your transit to be done at more reasonable prices increases as they can use their own workers instead of hiring laborers from outside such that it doesn't impact their profit as well as you get reduced costs.

Charges Incurred During Local Transit (Within City)

Household goods

The volume of your household goods decides the type of truck needed for your shifting. A truck can range from a small Tata Ace to a 32 feet Container. Price can range from Rs 1000 to 5000 and on the number of trips.

Packing

In a local shift, the transit time is hardly 1-3 hours; so the chances of your goods getting damaged are very negligible. Usually, packing is not done for all the items since the chances of damage are very negligible. Packing is done only for the fragile items and the electronic items and kitchen crockery is wrapped with papers and will be packed in cardboard boxes. Other big items like wardrobe, chairs and tables, etc are shifted without packing as the distance is very less. *You may tell the packers for proper packing of big items as per your wish.It may cost additional charges which depends on the quantity of packing material used. This kind of packing approximately costs around Rs 1000 to Rs 4000.

Manpower

Manpower includes the workers who are going to pack, load, unpack your goods. Professional workers usually charge Rs 700-800/person. Furthermore, below is the manpower required and costs incurred for a house shifting..

# Type of House Labor Requirement Price
1 1 BHK 2 Rs 1000-1600
2 2 BHK 3 Rs 1800-3000

Shifting Timings

In the city vehicle mobility is restricted from 8.00 am to 12.00 noon and 4.00 pm. to 9.00 pm. When you need to shift between these timings you need to pay a Challan of Rs 600.

Tips

Most of the shifting staff ask for a tip at the end. Although it totally depends on you whether you have to give or not. Usually they ask for Rs 50-100/person.

Additional Services

Usually, the undermentioned charges are not included in the quotation but can be arranged on the clients wish. Next Stop Packers Movers doesn't deal with assembling (Although we help in disassembling during packing) of the undermentioned items but we can arrange an electrician on the move day.

Charges Incurred During Interstate Transit (Outside City)

Distance

Although distance plays an important role in determining the cost of the transit; the place where the consignment is to be shipped also plays a significant role.

For example, If you are shifting a 2BHK from Mumbai to Bangalore, a shared truck would cost you around Rs 13k-20k. Also, If you are shifting the same 2BHK from Mumbai to Nagpur it would cost you around 15k to 22k. Even though the distance is the same, there's a difference in the charges.

Packing

Being aware of the conditions of the roads in India, it is essential to get your goods packed up properly to avoid any damages during the transit. Next Stop Packers Movers are known packers in the market for quality packing and secure transportation. So end your packers and movers search with us.

Packing may cost from Rs 3000 – 8000 for the standard items. If you have any special and delicate artifacts it can add up to Rs 10000

Unpacking

In Long Haul shifts, the facility of unpacking is not provided to the customers. Even though the facility of unloading of goods to their place is provided. The customers have to pack the goods by themselves. In case the customers need unpacking to be done, we can arrange for that but extra charges are applicable for this.

Unpacking can cost around Rs 1000 to 4000 depending upon the volume of goods.

Other Charges:

Since during interstate tansits the truck has to cross several borders so toll charges are applicable. In a few states octroi charges are applicable. These charges are not taken extra by the transport providers; they are included in the transportation costs.

Note- GST, Insurance(if opted), Stationery Charges, Mathadi,Union/ Green Tax,etc. would be extra.

Vehicle Requirement:

The transit cost also depends on the type of vehicle you opt for i.e. Shared or Direct/Separate.

If you opt for transit in sharing, the truck gets shared between 2 to 3 households and the delivery may take a little longer than usual as there would be 3 to 4 delivery locations. In this case, the costs are more reasonable.

If You opt for a Direct/Seperate truck, you get your shipment early but it will cost you additional Rs 5000 - 15000 more, based on the distance.

Insurance

Looking at the record of road accidents in India, it is advisable to you to opt for insurance of your goods. In case your goods get damaged during the transit due to some unavoidable issues, you can recover the amount using the insurance policy.

The Insurance amount usually costs around 3% of the total value of the goods declared by you.

Storage or Warehousing

In case the customers don't find a flat to stay in the new city/ state, Next Stop Packers Movers also provide storage and warehousing services at minimal costs. The first 15days storage is free of cost and after that some charges start to occur.

Furthermore, if customers wish to move abroad for some days they can store their goods at our warehouses. The charges would be as per the agreement.

Next Stop Packers Movers charges a sum of Rs 5000/- per month for storage or warehousing of goods.
