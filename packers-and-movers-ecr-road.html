Expert Packing Tips for a Smooth Move

To help you prepare for your upcoming relocation in Ecr Road, our experts have compiled a few essential tips:

  • Declutter First: Don't pay to move things you no longer need. Donate or sell items that haven't been used in over a year.
  • Essentials Box: Pack a "First Night" box containing toiletries, basic tools, chargers, and a change of clothes.
  • Label Everything: Clearly mark boxes with the room they belong to (e.g., "Kitchen - Fragile") to make unpacking easier.
  • Keep Documents Safe: Always carry your personal documents, jewelry, and expensive electronics with you rather than loading them in the truck.
